## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI speeds up coding but still makes subtle mistakes that only an experienced engineer can spot. It amplifies good developers and misleads beginners.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Engineers will spend less time writing code and more time reviewing and directing AI. Core fundamentals will matter more, not less.

## My hot take on AI
