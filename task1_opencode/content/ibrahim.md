## What I think about AI in software engineering today
I find AI helpful across the software development process. However, I am concerned that making new software easier to build may encourage teams to replace existing solutions that still work well. I think such changes should bring a clear benefit.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
I expect software to be used in more areas, with developers increasingly designing systems and assembling AI-generated components. Many existing solutions will be replaced along the way, which I have mixed feelings about. Progress should also mean recognising when an existing solution is worth keeping.

## My hot take on AI
Just because AI can build a replacement does not mean the old software needs replacing.
