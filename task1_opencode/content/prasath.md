## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI in software engineering has evolved from a simple autocomplete tool into an essential, code-generating layer that handles the bulk of routine implementation while shifting human work toward architecture and verification.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
software engineering will shift from manually writing code to designing, governing, and verifying autonomous software systems.While the total volume of software in the world will explode, human engineers will spend almost zero time typing syntax and nearly all of their time defining system intent, managing risk, and owning business outcomes.

## My hot take on AI
The real AI bubble isn't the technology itself, it's the belief that throwing autonomous agents at messy organizations will somehow fix bad product strategy and broken processes.
