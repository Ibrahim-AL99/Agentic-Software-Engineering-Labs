Our shared working perspective

AI gets the autocomplete. Humans keep the steering wheel.

Across five different takes, a common thread: AI can speed up everyday implementation, but people still need to understand, review, and direct the systems—and decide what is worth building or replacing. As routine code gets easier to generate, our profiles point toward more human attention on architecture, verification, product choices, and real-world outcomes. The tools can help; responsibility for the result stays with us. (And yes, somebody still has to review that “tiny” change.)
