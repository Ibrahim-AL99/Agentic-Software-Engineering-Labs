The Future of Software Engineering

We expect AI agents to take on more routine implementation, testing, and maintenance, while people devote more attention to intent, architecture, and system design. This will make the ability to evaluate generated work—not just produce it—more important than ever.

Software engineering will still need human judgment: teams must ask what to build, verify that it works, consider the risks, and take responsibility for its impact. Our shared view is that AI can make capable engineers more effective, but it cannot replace clear thinking, sound fundamentals, or accountability.
